Support for Xe Xưa

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Xe Xưa: Đua Xe Máy is a motorbike racing game set on the streets and country roads of Vietnam in years gone by. You ride old mopeds and motorbikes through traffic in endless runs, stage races and delivery missions.

How to play

  • The bike accelerates by itself. Hold the left or right half of the screen to steer.
  • PHANH is the brake.
  • Passing other vehicles closely, overtaking, and riding in the oncoming lanes fill the VẶN HẾT GA gauge. When it is full, tap it for a burst of extra speed.
  • Hitting a vehicle, a tree, a pole or a house front is a crash. In an endless run you have three lives.
  • Race stages: finish in the top three to unlock the next stage.
  • Missions: reach the goal before the clock runs out. Some cargo breaks if you crash.
  • Spend the money you earn in the garage (GARA) on new bikes and upgrades.
  • Tap the pause button, or press the Android back button, to pause a ride.

Common questions

I don't hear any sound

Check the speaker button at the top left of the main menu, or the sound button on the pause screen, and make sure your device isn't in silent mode.

My progress disappeared

Money, bikes, upgrades and stars are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Something's broken, or you have feedback

Please include your device model and system version (for example "Redmi Note 13, Android 14") and describe what happened. It helps a lot in tracking issues down.
