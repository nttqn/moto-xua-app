## English

**Title / Name**

Xe Xua: Vintage Bike Racing

**App Store subtitle**

**Google Play short description**

**App Store promotional text**

**App Store keywords**

motorbike,moped,racing,traffic,vietnam,retro,vintage,saigon,hanoi,rider,delivery,endless,bike

**Full description**

Kick-start an old moped and ride the roads of Vietnam as they used to be.

Xe Xua is a behind-the-bike racing game. You weave between bicycles, cyclos, three-wheelers, country buses, trucks and the odd ox cart, down streets of narrow shop-houses, hand-painted signs and tangled power lines.

EASY TO PLAY, ONE HAND
• The bike accelerates by itself. Hold the left or right half of the screen to steer.
• One brake button, one full-throttle button.
• Pass close, overtake, and ride into the oncoming lanes to fill the throttle gauge. When it is full, open it up: the bike surges and the pipe spits fire.
• Hit a vehicle, a tree, a pole, a street stall or a house front and you are down.

FOUR MODES
• Endless: three lives. The farther you ride and the closer you pass, the higher the score.
• Stage races: 12 stages against five rival riders.
• Missions: 16 jobs such as the morning paper run, a block of ice, a pig for the market, a wedding party that must not be late. Some cargo is lost the first time you fall.
• Garage: buy bikes and upgrade engine, exhaust, tyres and throttle.

SEVEN BIKES
From a gentle little step-through to a two-stroke sports bike and a legendary old racer. Each one handles differently: some are easy, some are quick but run wide, and the fastest has to be wrestled through every bend.

FIVE REGIONS
Saigon streets, tree-lined Hanoi, the highway through the rice fields, mountain passes, and the palm-lined roads of the Mekong delta, by day and at dusk.

OLD-TIME FEEL
• Poster-style art and a bolero menu theme.
• An engine note that follows the throttle.
• Plays offline.

Free to play, with ads. The game is in Vietnamese.
